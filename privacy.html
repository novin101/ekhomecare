Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Ever Kind Home Care is a non-medical home care organization (California HCO License #304700537). We collect only the information needed to answer your questions about care, and we never sell it.

Information we collect

When you contact us through our website, a Facebook or Instagram lead form, phone or email, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, email address and city or ZIP code
  • Who the care is for and when you would like care to start
  • Anything you choose to tell us in a message about the help you are looking for

Please don't share medical records or detailed health information through our forms. We will talk through your needs by phone.

How we use your information

  • To call or email you back about home care services
  • To check whether we serve your area and arrange a free consultation
  • To understand which of our ads and pages are helpful, so we can improve them

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with services that help us run our business, and only as needed:

  • Formspree delivers website form submissions to our email.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram) provides our lead forms and ads. If you submit a lead form on Facebook or Instagram, Meta shares your answers with us.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

Cookies and advertising

Our website uses the Meta Pixel, which uses cookies and similar technology. It tells us when someone visits a page, taps our phone number or sends a request. This helps us measure our ads and show them to people who may need our services. When you send a request, your email address and phone number may be shared with Meta in a scrambled (hashed) form for this purpose. We never send Meta your name or the content of your message.

You can control ad personalization in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings, and block cookies in your browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information only as long as we need it to respond to you and provide services, or as required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, at any time. Email admin@everkindhc.com or call (949) 391-3041.

Children

Our website and forms are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new date.
