Home care services

Everyday support, given with kindness.

Our caregivers provide non-medical home care that helps your loved one stay safe, comfortable and independent at home.

Companionship

Friendly conversation, activities, supervision, emotional support and help reducing isolation.

Personal Care

Help with daily routines, dressing, hygiene, grooming, mobility support and reminders.

Meal Preparation

Preparing meals, encouraging hydration and supporting healthy daily eating routines.

Light Housekeeping

Tidying, laundry, dishes and household organization to keep home comfortable.

Errands & Appointments

Shopping, errands, getting to appointments, reminders and safe day-to-day routines.

Respite Care

Dependable temporary relief for family caregivers who need rest or an extra pair of hands.

Ever Kind provides non-medical home care. We do not provide skilled nursing, medical treatment or emergency medical services.

Questions

Home care questions, answered.

Common questions families ask before starting non-medical home care.

What type of care does Ever Kind Home Care provide?

We provide non-medical home care, including companionship, personal care, meal preparation, light housekeeping, errands, appointments, respite care and daily living support.

What areas do you serve?

We serve families throughout Orange County, including Mission Viejo, Lake Forest, Rancho Santa Margarita, Irvine, Laguna Niguel, Aliso Viejo, Dana Point, San Juan Capistrano, Laguna Hills, Laguna Woods and nearby communities.

How do we get started?

Call us at (949) 391-3041 to discuss your loved one's needs. We'll talk through the situation and help identify the right type of care support.

Is this medical care?

No. Ever Kind Home Care provides non-medical home care. We do not provide skilled nursing, medical treatment or emergency medical services.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Ever Kind is a California Licensed Home Care Organization, License #304700537, and we are bonded and insured.
