Respite Care: A Break for Family Caregivers

Caring for a parent or spouse is a labor of love, and everyone needs time to rest. Ever Kind Home Care sends a caregiver to your loved one's home so you can take a few hours, a day or a trip, knowing they have company and help.

When You Need Respite Care

Many families in Mission Viejo, Laguna Woods and nearby cities look after an aging loved one themselves. Respite care helps when you need to work, see a doctor of your own, travel, care for your children, or simply rest.

Respite care can be occasional or regular: a few hours a week, a set day each week, or extra help while an adult son or daughter is out of town. We talk with you about the schedule and the kind of help your loved one needs.

Non-medical care only: we help with companionship and everyday routines. We do not provide skilled nursing or medical treatment.

How a Respite Caregiver Helps

During a respite visit, your loved one gets the same non-medical support you would give them.

Companionship

Conversation, games, walks and activities, so your loved one has friendly company while you're away.

Daily Routines

Help with dressing, grooming, getting around the home and daily reminders.

Meals

Preparing meals and snacks and encouraging good hydration.

Light Housekeeping

Tidying, dishes and laundry, so you come home to a calm house.

Errands & Outings

Grocery runs, errands and rides to appointments or social activities.

Peace of Mind

Someone attentive is there, and we keep the family informed about how the visit went.

All of our services are non-medical. We do not provide skilled nursing, medical treatment or emergency medical services.

Respite Care Questions

Common questions from family caregivers in South Orange County.

What is respite care?

Respite care is short-term or occasional help that gives a family caregiver a break. Our caregiver supports your loved one at home with non-medical help such as companionship, meals, light housekeeping and daily routines.

Can respite care be occasional?

Yes. It can be a few hours here and there, regular weekly visits, or help while a family member is away. Call (949) 391-3041 to talk through the schedule you have in mind.

How much does respite care cost?

Rates depend on the type of help needed and the schedule. Call us for a free consultation and we'll go over pricing with you.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve South Orange County, including Mission Viejo, Laguna Woods, Laguna Hills, Lake Forest, Aliso Viejo, Laguna Niguel, Rancho Santa Margarita, San Juan Capistrano, Dana Point and Irvine.

Is this medical care?

No. Ever Kind Home Care provides non-medical home care only. We do not provide skilled nursing, medical treatment or emergency medical services.

Is Ever Kind Home Care licensed?

Yes. Ever Kind Home Care is a California Licensed Home Care Organization, HCO License #304700537.
